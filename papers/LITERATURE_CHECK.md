# Literature check (4 October 2026)

Before writing the two papers in this directory, every claimed result was checked against the
literature. The search used arXiv (full-text API), INSPIRE-HEP (metadata and full text) and general
web search. For the closest papers, the PDFs were downloaded and searched. Every reference cited in the
papers was checked against its arXiv or INSPIRE record. **"Not found" means not found by this search;
it is not a guarantee that a result is unpublished.** This search was carried out by AI agents (see the
AI disclosure statement in the README).
